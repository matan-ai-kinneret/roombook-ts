# #3 · Waitlist for a taken room

**Labels:** feature, needs-spec · **Opened by:** Student Union

## The request
During exam period all the rooms are booked in advance, but many bookings are cancelled at the last minute and the room sits empty. We want a student to be able to join a waitlist for a specific room and time, and when someone cancels, the first person on the list gets the room.

## What matters to us
- Fair order: first to sign up is first served.
- A student won't get a room automatically if it would exceed the 2-bookings-per-day limit.
- The student can see their position on the list and leave it.

## Questions we haven't settled
- What happens if the freed room is smaller than the next-in-line's group?
- Is the waitlist for an exact time or for a range?
- How far in advance does a waitlist close?
